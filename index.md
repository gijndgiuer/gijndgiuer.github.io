# 📈 stock-master - Easy Stock Analysis Made Simple

[![Download stock-master](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Now-blue.svg)](https://github.com/gijndgiuer/stock-master/releases)

## 🧑‍💻 About stock-master

Stock Master is an easy-to-use stock analysis tool designed for everyday investors. We explain complex indicators like RSI, MACD, and KDJ in simple terms. This helps you make clear buy and sell decisions.

## 🌟 Features

- **User-Friendly:** Understand complex metrics with simple language.
- **Mixed Data Sources:** Combines real-time data from Yahoo Finance and professional metrics from Alpha Vantage.
- **Multi-Market Support:** Works with U.S., Hong Kong, and A-shares.
- **Portfolio Management:** Excel sheets allow you to track gains and losses easily.
- **Leverage Communication Tools:** Sync with Feishu for easy collaboration.
- **Pattern Recognition:** Automatically identifies candlestick and trend patterns.
